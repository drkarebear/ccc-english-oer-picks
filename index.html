A small, open faculty project

CCC English OER Picks is created, reviewed, and maintained by one California Community College English faculty member, Karen Crozer. It is an independent volunteer project, not an official ASCCC OERI or CCCCO resource.

Faculty input is welcome: recommend resources, share teaching experience, suggest corrections, review draft rubrics, or volunteer for a larger role.

See how the reviews work

The ratings are meant to be inspectable. The site publishes the course criteria, explains the AI-assisted first pass and human review, and keeps faculty experience separate from editorial alignment.

COURSE RUBRICS

See exactly what each course-alignment rating is based on.

Each course has its own working rubric built around the instructional work of that course rather than a generic textbook checklist.

AI + HUMAN REVIEW

See how an OER moves from a first pass to a published observation.

AI helps scan large resources and draft criterion-level findings. I then open the resource, check the evidence, revise unsupported claims or scores, and make the final editorial judgment.

FACULTY EXPERIENCE

Keep classroom experience visible as a different kind of evidence.

Faculty recommendations and teaching notes are not folded into the alignment score. They can reinforce, complicate, or correct what an editorial review suggests.

This project is open to your input.

You do not need to join a committee or take on an ongoing role to contribute. A useful contribution can be as small as one teaching note, one correction, one rubric comment, or one OER recommendation.

Have another perspective?
