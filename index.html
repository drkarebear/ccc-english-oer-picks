A small, open faculty project

CCC English OER Picks is currently created, reviewed, and maintained by one California Community College English faculty member, Karen Crozer. It is an independent volunteer project, not an official ASCCC OERI or CCCCO resource.

Faculty input is welcome: recommend resources, share teaching experience, suggest corrections, review draft rubrics or IDEA crosswalks, or volunteer for a larger role.

This project is open to your input.

You do not need to join a committee or take on an ongoing role to contribute. A useful contribution can be as small as one teaching note, one correction, one rubric comment, or one OER recommendation.

Used one of these OERs?

Share a faculty recommendation or teaching note from real classroom use.

See something differently?

Teach another English course?

Help review a future COR-based rubric or suggest a course for the rubric library.

Interested in IDEA?

Review an IDEA crosswalk or help refine how that lens is applied to OER.

Found an OER we missed?

Recommend a resource that should be considered for one of the course guides.
