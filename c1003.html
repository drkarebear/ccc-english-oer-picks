How we look at C1003 alignment

The editorial review considers eight C1003-specific areas: literary breadth, close reading and literary analysis, argument and thesis development, logic and reasoning, biases and fallacies, critical approaches and context, research and source evaluation, and writing, revision, and documentation.

C1003 is not simply an Introduction to Literature course and not simply a critical-thinking course. Strong resources should help faculty teach students to make and evaluate arguments through sustained engagement with literature. Many effective courses will intentionally pair a literature-centered core with a focused argument or reasoning supplement.

Scores reflect explicit instructional coverage, not simply opportunities for students to practice a skill. Three dots means substantial, teachable instruction in that area; two dots means meaningful instruction is present but supplementation may be appropriate; one dot means the resource touches the area or supports practice without substantial direct instruction. A zero does not mean the resource is poor—it usually means that is not what the resource is designed to teach.

These are independent editorial assessments, not ASCCC/OERI ratings or endorsements.
