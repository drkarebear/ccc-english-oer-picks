Privacy

Privacy Statement

CCC English OER Picks is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to operate the site and support trustworthy faculty contributions.

The short version

Your email address is used for account authentication and site integrity. It is not displayed publicly, sold, or used for marketing. Reviews, recommendations, teaching notes, and OER suggestions you choose to submit may be stored and, where appropriate, displayed publicly.

Information you provide

If you choose to sign in, CCC English OER Picks collects your email address to authenticate your account, distinguish individual contributors, reduce duplicate or automated submissions, and help maintain the integrity of faculty reviews.

Your email address is not displayed publicly.

If you submit a faculty review, recommendation, teaching note, or OER suggestion, that information may be stored by the site and, where appropriate, displayed publicly. Your email address is not published with those contributions.

Technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for authentication and database services. These service providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, authentication events, and request or activity information as part of operating and securing their services.

How information is used

Information collected directly by CCC English OER Picks is used to operate the site, authenticate contributors, moderate submissions, reduce duplicate or automated activity, and maintain the integrity of faculty recommendations and teaching notes.

No marketing or sale of information. CCC English OER Picks does not sell personal information and does not use email addresses for marketing or mailing lists.

Service providers

GitHub provides website hosting, and Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure. Information processed by those services is also subject to their respective privacy practices.

CCC English OER Picks does not currently use its own advertising trackers, behavioral advertising tools, or third-party analytics services.

Questions, corrections, and deletion requests

You may contact the project to ask about information you have provided or request correction or deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons.

Contact: karencrozer@gmail.com

Changes to this statement

If the project's privacy practices materially change, this page will be updated to describe those changes.
