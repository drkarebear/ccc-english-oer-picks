How I review C1002 Content Alignment

I apply the C1002 Content Alignment Rubric to the instructional content of each resource. The eight areas are literary breadth, poetry, drama, fiction, diverse literary voices, literary terms and critical approaches, close reading and literary analysis, and research and writing about literature.

Strong Alignment means substantial, explicit instruction; Meaningful Alignment means useful direct instruction that may need supplementation; Limited Alignment means the resource touches the area or provides some instruction but not sustained coverage; and Not a Significant Focus means little or no meaningful instructional treatment. A low rating in one area does not mean the OER is poor. It may simply be designed for a different role.

I am currently the sole evaluator for these editorial Content Alignment reviews. Faculty Experience is collected separately and never changes the Content Alignment ratings. The IDEA Framework review is also a separate lens.

These are independent editorial assessments, not ASCCC/OERI ratings or endorsements.
